# Conjectures

Proposed mathematical statements that are believed to hold but have not been
proven. A conjecture earns a page here once it is precise enough to state
formally and to test computationally.

A page in this section should carry:

- a formal statement, with every quantifier and side condition made explicit;
- the evidence for it — which [experiments](../results/index.md) were run, over
  what range, and what they found;
- known special cases that are already proven, and where they live;
- what a proof would need, and which obstructions are known.

Once a conjecture is proven, its page moves to [Theorems](../theorems/index.md)
and the Lean formalization lands in `src/theorems/`, surfacing in the
[Lean API](../reference/lean/index.md).
